Culture, Events and Festivals

Customs and etiquette

Dress

Brunei is a conservative Islamic country, so visitors are asked to dress modestly in pubic. Tank tops, shorts and short skirts are best avoided, and covering your shoulders and knees is the usual guidance.

Mosques

Take your shoes off before you go into a mosque, and check visiting times first, since access can change around prayer times.

Everyday manners

Be careful when talking about religion or the royal family. Use your right hand to give and receive things and to eat.

Sources: Singapore Ministry of Foreign Affairs: Brunei travel page, Visit Southeast Asia: advice for visitors to Brunei

Events and festivals

National Day: 23 February

Marks Brunei's independence from Britain in 1984, with parades and celebrations across the country.

Armed Forces Day: 31 May

Celebrates the founding of the Royal Brunei Armed Forces in 1961, with military displays and exhibitions.

Sultan's Birthday: 15 July

A public holiday for the birthday of Sultan Hassanal Bolikah, and one of the biggest dates in the national calendar.
