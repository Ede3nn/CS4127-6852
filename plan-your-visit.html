Getting there and around

Brunei International Airport is only about a 15-minute drive from the capital, and several airlines fly there, including Royal Brunei. If you are coming from Malaysia, you can also arrive by road through one of the land entry points. Brunei Tourism: How to get to Brunei

Entry and visa information

Entry requirements depend on your nationality and can change, so check the official visa information before you book anything. Brunei Tourism: Visa information

Accommodation

You can stay in hotels, serviced apartments, motels or lodges and resots, and the official tourism site lets you browse each type. Brunei Tourism: Accommodation

Best time to visit

Brunei is hot and humid all year, so pack light clothes and an umbrella whatever the month. Some guies say there is no clearly good or bad season. Hotels can get busy around big dates like the Sultan's birthday on 15 July. World Travel Guide: Brunei weather and climate
