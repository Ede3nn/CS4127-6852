The land of peace, Brunei

Gold domes, a rainforest that has barely been touched, and a water village that has stood for centuries. Brunei is small, calm and easy to get around.

Where it is

Brunei sits on the north coast of Borneo, bordered by Sarawak in Malaysia and the South China Sea. The capital is Bandar Seri Begawan.

Why go

Low crime

Quiet streets, friendly people and very little hassle for visitors. Serious crime is rare, according to Australia Smartraveller. Australia's Smartraveller

Real Rainforest

Primary forest in Ulu Temburong, reached by a boat.
